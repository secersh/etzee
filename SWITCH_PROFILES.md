# Switch Profiles

Mechanical stackup parameters per switch family. Used to drive OnShape configurations and PCB matrix variants.

| Parameter | MX Clones | Choc v2 | KS-33 |
|-----------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Switch plate thickness (mm) | 1.5 – 1.9 | 1.65 | 1.2 |
| Carrier board thickness (mm) | 1.6 | 1.6 | 1.5 – 1.6 |
| Top plate → top carrier offset (mm) | 5.0 | 2.2 | 2.5 |

**Offset** is the vertical distance from the top surface of the switch plate to the top surface of the carrier board.
