# Switch Compatibility

| Switch | Manufacturer | Profile | Socket | PCB Variant |
|--------|-------------|---------|--------|-------------|
| MX | Cherry | Normal | PG151101S11 | `-N` |
| MX-compatible | Kailh, Gateron, others | Normal | PG151101S11 | `-N` |
| Choc v2 (PG1353) | Kailh | Low | CPG135001S30 | `-L` |
| KS-33 (KS-2P02B01-01-a) | Gateron | Low | KS-2P02B01-01-a | `-L` |

All switches share a 19.2 mm × 19.2 mm grid pitch and 14 × 14 mm plate cutout.
